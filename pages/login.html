Student account help

Forgot your password? Choose Forgot Password? on the login form and enter your sign-in email. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset link.

Use a username? Recover with your backup key or use a reset code from your current teacher.

Need help getting in or requesting a copy, correction, or deletion? Ask a parent or guardian to help you speak with the school or adult who gave you access. LakbayPanahon has no public support inbox or request form yet.

If you can sign in, go to Settings → Account help for your learning-data download and things you can change yourself. Keep passwords, reset links, and downloaded files private.
