Help for students and guardians

For help getting in or requesting a copy, correction, or deletion of your data, ask a parent or guardian to speak with the school or adult who gave you access. LakbayPanahon has no public support inbox or request form yet.

When signed in, go to Settings → Account help to download your learning data. Keep passwords, reset links, and downloaded files private.
