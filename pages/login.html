Email hasn’t arrived?

  • Check Spam or Junk. Search your inbox for “Reset your password” or “LakbayPanahon”.
  • Check the address above. If you used a different email to sign up, enter that address and request a link.
  • Use the latest reset email. If its link expired or was already used, request a fresh one.
  • If you can no longer open that inbox, ask the school or adult who helped you register for guidance.

Help for students and guardians

For help getting in or requesting a copy, correction, or deletion of your data, ask a parent or guardian to speak with the school or adult who gave you access. LakbayPanahon has no public support inbox or request form yet.

When signed in, go to Settings → Account help to download your learning data. Keep passwords, reset links, and downloaded files private.
