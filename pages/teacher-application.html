How to prepare your evidence and who can see it

Cover addresses, birth dates, signatures, and unrelated numbers unless needed for the review. Do not upload student records, passwords, or an unredacted full government ID.

Only you and authorized administrators can access your qualification details and evidence. Image access expires after 30 days. Images are removed after a review decision and during expired-evidence cleanup. Qualification details and review decisions remain until the account-data process removes them.

Saving a draft does not send your application for review.
