Good to know

What does my account save?

Your account saves your name, sign-in email and chosen avatar. As you learn, it also saves lesson progress, quiz answers and results, practice progress, rewards, saved clues and class membership.

My Journey shows your learning progress. My Profile shows your account details.

Does logging out delete my progress?

No. Log Out signs you out of the website. Progress already saved to your account remains for your next visit. Check that your activity has finished saving before you leave.

Log out when you finish on a shared computer. Keep your password private.

What does Reset to defaults change?

It resets the sound, music, motion, contrast and reading choices in this browser. These choices can stay on a shared computer after you log out.

Resetting preferences does not delete your account, saved clues or learning progress.
