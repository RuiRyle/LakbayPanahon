Teacher setup

From account to classroom

Classroom tools become available once an administrator approves your application.

  1. Create an account

    Enter your sign-in details and confirm your email address.

  2. Apply for classroom access

    Provide your teaching details and check your application status after signing in.

  3. Invite your students

    Once approved, create a class and share its code. Students enter it in My Class.

Teacher FAQs

Where do I check my application?

Log in with your teacher account to resume setup or check your application status.

Do students need a teacher account?

Students use their own student accounts. They enter the code you share in My Class to join your classroom.

Does mission completion show mastery?

Completion shows which missions a student has finished. Review saved quiz attempts alongside completion to decide what to discuss or practice next.
