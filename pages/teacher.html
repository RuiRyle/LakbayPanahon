YOUR ROLE IN LAKBAYPANAHON

Guide, Monitor, and Support

LakbayPanahon is primarily an independent learning platform for Grade 5 students. Teachers provide optional classroom support by creating private classes and monitoring students who choose to join them.

The main Philippine History lessons, missions, and quizzes are already part of LakbayPanahon, allowing teachers to focus on supporting student learning rather than managing the system's learning content.
