# Prologue Mission 2 — formation learning pilot

Published to original Firestore project `lakbaypanahon-1797e` on 27 October 2026. No student records, reward values, prerequisite settings or quiz passing rules were changed.

The six-scene sequence introduces a mission problem, follows plate-movement arrows, works through volcanic accumulation, compares shallow exposure with a flooded deep channel, evaluates evidence, distinguishes scientific and traditional-story questions, and ends with a synthesis recap. Three observation cards per scene add information rather than copy the main paragraphs verbatim. Choices and explanatory feedback use the existing story interaction; these are illustrated comparison tasks, not a physical simulation or draggable diagram.

Four complete SVG illustrations are included in `assets/story/`: `formation-plates.svg`, `formation-volcano.svg`, `formation-shoreline.svg` and `formation-evidence.svg`. Equivalent descriptions are stored in each scene's `imageAlt`. `imageFit: contain` keeps the labels visible. Models are clearly labelled as simplified, not to scale, not maps and not eruption forecasts. The shoreline panels share the exact same land outline so students can distinguish exposed existing land from new rock. No claim is made that every Philippine island was connected by a land bridge. Source-comparison cards do not invent or reproduce an authentic community origin story.

Physical explanations are informed by [PHIVOLCS](https://www.phivolcs.dost.gov.ph/introduction-to-volcanoes/), [USGS plate tectonics](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/volc/tectonics.html), [NOAA ocean volcanoes](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/volcanoes.html) and the [NOAA repository coastal-ocean reference](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/39955/noaa_39955_DS1.pdf). These sources do not constitute teacher approval or current-curriculum certification.

`data/formation-pilot-lessons.json` and `data/formation-pilot-practice.json` are authoring inputs. `functions/build-formation-pilot.cjs` builds a guarded 27-document migration: six scenes (one new), mission teaching metadata, ten main-quiz variants and ten distinct practice prompts. Existing question IDs, topic IDs and review-evidence policies remain. Runtime content comes from Firestore; assets are local static illustrations referenced by scene fields. Run `functions/apply-formation-pilot.cjs` without arguments for preflight; `--apply` backs up, commits atomically and verifies all fields. The original snapshot is a precondition baseline; do not refresh it and rebuild to bypass an editorial collision.
