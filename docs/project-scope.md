# Agreed LakbayPanahon scope

Reconciled with the user's supplied revised study summary on 31 October 2026. Later explicit user decisions take precedence over older architectural suggestions.

LakbayPanahon is an independent, AI-powered, gamified Philippine History learning system for Grade 5 students. It supports selected relevant Araling Panlipunan history topics, not a claim of complete national curriculum coverage. It supplements instruction. A student can register and learn without a school subscription or class membership.

The student scope includes account/profile/avatar management; interactive lessons; story missions; a historical timeline; quizzes and performance-based adaptive review; Bayani explanations and generated review questions; XP, badges and artifacts; saved progress; and optional class membership and published announcements. Generated AI practice is not authoritative grading evidence or a source of XP.

The user's subsequent feature request also authorizes History Detective Cases, Then and Now comparisons, richer earned-artifact museum exhibits, a community illustration that grows from mission completions, and authored Explain It to Bayani practice. The initial case content covers three named missions; the optional activities supplement the existing lessons/quizzes and do not award official progress or XP. Community decorations derive from saved mission completions. This does not authorize automatically fabricated questions for every mission or a new paid AI dependency.

Teachers are optional monitors/facilitators: approved teacher accounts, classes/codes, joined-student progress/results and announcements. Teachers do not author the main curriculum. Administrators manage platform accounts and teacher verification. These roles do not become prerequisites for independent student learning.

The current authorized implementation is the existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript website and Firebase project. The older Flutter/React architecture in the summary is historical planning, not authorization to rewrite the website. Android/iOS work is outside this current website task.

The evaluation concerns functionality, usability, satisfaction and acceptance among selected participants. Cebu is the proposed pilot location, not an application access restriction. Do not claim long-term learning gains or national effectiveness from code checks or a small pilot.

Students and potential institutional buyers are distinct. Buyer/school/operator details remain undecided and do not block development. Free independent access and optional classroom support remain central. Premium features and optional rewarded advertising were business-model ideas, not permission to add payments or advertising now.

Use this scope when assessing completion. Full recorded narration, provider/account eligibility, actual AI replies, content accuracy, intended learner feedback, real-device output and hosted acceptance each need their own evidence. Do not substitute a demonstration-only scope or expand selected-topic scope into an entire-curriculum promise.

On 31 October the user requested suitable, enjoyable learning enhancements across every existing mission with DepEd curriculum alignment. This authorizes the 66 mission-specific learning guides/application activities, shared short-reading and glossary improvements, and reviewable curriculum mappings/content plans. Mapping uses the inspected Grade 5 MATATAG August 2023 baseline provisionally; it is not DepEd approval, proof of learning or a claim of superiority to school instruction. Existing depth gaps must remain visible until substantively reviewed.
