## questions/chap2-quiz2-question5

The Manunggul Jar from Palawan and the different traditions of Philippine communities teach us which important lesson?

Correct answer: D

Key idea: Beliefs and practices were diverse; artifacts provide evidence that requires careful interpretation across different periods.

- **A: Every early Filipino community followed exactly the same customs.** ? The lesson emphasizes differences among communities, so identical customs everywhere is too broad a claim.
- **B: Only one community in the Philippines had spiritual beliefs.** ? The lesson describes spiritual beliefs in different communities, not just one community.
- **C: Artifacts can tell us everything about the past without interpretation.** ? Artifacts offer clues, but their meaning and historical context require interpretation rather than revealing everything directly.
- **D: Communities had diverse beliefs, and artifacts can provide clues about their traditions.** ? This combines both ideas: communities had diverse traditions, and artifacts offer evidence that must be interpreted carefully. The Manunggul Jar is from an earlier period than some communities in this mission.
