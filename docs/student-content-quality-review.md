## Findings that need action

1. **High priority: hard-question answer pattern.** There are 225 active main adaptive-hard records with the identical correct answer: "The note supports the point it states in this case; broader conclusions need more evidence." These items mostly ask students to recognize a safe generic conclusion rather than apply the particular history topic. Replace them with concrete topic-specific comparisons, causes, chronology or source tasks and plausible misconceptions. Preserve quiz keys and adaptive policy through guarded authoring; unchanged difficulty labels still need instructional review.
2. **High priority: misleading completion notices.** Twenty-two lesson endings across Chapters 8-12 say later missions or challenges are unfinished. Every affected chapter has an active challenge in the reviewed catalog. Correct the availability statements while preserving the rule that a mission alone does not award the chapter badge.
3. **Medium priority: insufficiently diagnostic feedback.** There are 1,054 repeated-wrong-feedback flags across main/practice banks. Much feedback repeats one general explanation regardless of the selected answer. Explain the actual misconception, then point to the relevant clue or example. These items already have feedback; this is a quality issue, not missing feedback fields.
4. **Medium priority: reading load and lesson balance.** There are 162 paragraphs over 70 words, with a maximum of 118. Short-reading pagination already preserves the wording, so these are not necessarily inaccessible screens. Edit repetition, define technical words beside examples and retain specific historical knowledge. Do not remove named events merely to lower word counts.

## What the counts do and do not mean

There are 46 missions outside the deeper Chapter 3-7 revision batch; 45 have at least one of the existing audit flags. This does not mean 46 broken missions or prove all 46 need a complete rewrite. The unflagged Life in the Barangay mission already has earlier clarity work and still needs normal reviewer judgment. The 450 generic-stem flags include the 225 hard questions above; feedback and paragraph flags overlap. They cannot be added as a count of distinct defects.

All 330 assessed topics have structural lesson mapping. Missing practice-feedback maps and missing scene-reference lists were repaired earlier. These passes do not prove that each explanation teaches its topic well or that every cited source supports every statement. Thirteen curriculum depth findings remain open.

## Representative editorial review

- **Chapter 8, missionary aims:** the hard item gives a lesson note, then rewards the same generic statement about the note's scope. A stronger task would distinguish missionary intent from evidence of local response in a specific provided case, without assessing a student's faith.
- **Chapter 9, polo y servicios:** the lesson already explains required labor and distinguishes a work demand from evidence that work occurred. Keep this substantive distinction. Move repetitive classroom instructions out of the main explanation where possible, and apply the distinction to a specific evidence task.
- **Chapter 10, social hierarchy:** the lesson explains privilege and correctly avoids treating status as human worth. It needs a concrete labelled record linking a category to a duty or opportunity so the student learns more than the instruction to avoid generalization. Do not turn sensitivity safeguards into the whole lesson.
- **Chapter 12, regional uprisings:** the supplied reading already names Tamblot/Bohol/1621, Sumuroy/Palapag/1649 and Basi/Ilocos/1807. Preserve and verify these specific cases; compare their causes and outcomes rather than repeating only that cases differ. The historical dates were not independently re-verified in this editorial review.
- **Chapter 14, women's participation:** the supplied Gabriela Silang example gives a named contribution. Keep the contribution, connect it to supporting evidence and avoid allowing general "a source has limits" answers to replace knowledge of that contribution. This is not a new factual certification.
