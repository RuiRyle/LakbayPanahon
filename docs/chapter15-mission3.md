## Learning sequence

Six scenes teach the parish community and responsibilities, secular/regular
clergy categories, Pedro Pelaez's advocacy, secularization and Filipinization,
a role–concern–request case board, and a chronological recap. Five topics have
ten medium main-question variants and ten separate easy/hard practice questions.
Normal mission assessment retains five questions, passing at three correct,
50 XP and a mission artifact awarded once.

The vocabulary explicitly distinguishes secular clergy from having no religion,
and regular clergy from frequency of attendance. Clergy organization, religious
belief and nationality are not treated as interchangeable. The mission asks
students to study institutions, not perform a religious practice or disclose
beliefs. Diagrams are modern teaching aids rather than historic records.

The Pelaez example uses the NCCA account of an 1861 parish-transfer objection.
Its chronology distinguishes his 1863 death during the Manila earthquake from
later events of 1872. There is no graphic depiction or reenactment of death.
The lesson does not equate an objection with proof that a reform succeeded.

## Reform case board

The existing data-driven exhibit mechanic now accepts optional authored
`eyebrow` and `matchPrompt` strings. Generic labels also fit roles and concerns,
not only contributions. Correct IDs and explanations stay in Firestore data.

Students explore the clues, connect a responsibility, a documented concern and
a proposed change, then explain the connection. Wrong matches offer guidance;
confirmed and partial matches resume after refresh. Revising the board clears
the scene's explanation requirement so it must be completed again.

## Sources and limits

- [NCCA CulturEd: Pedro Pelaez](https://philippineculturaleducation.com.ph/pelaez-pedro/): advocacy, the 1861 objection and later context.
- [Loyola School of Theology publication description](https://publications.lst.edu/product/pedro-pelaez-brebes-apuntes-sobre-la-cuestion-de-curatos-de-filipinas-a-transcription/): confirms the subject of Pelaez's written advocacy. Only the public editorial description was consulted, not the login-restricted full transcription.
- [Catholic Encyclopedia: Secular Clergy](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13675a.htm): historical terminology, simplified for this lesson. It is not used as a source for Philippine event chronology or a modern legal ruling.

The case-board sentences are modern summaries, not invented historical
quotations. Teacher, historical and learner-comprehension review remains pending.
The mission does not claim a complete institutional history.
