## Learning sequence

Six illustrated scenes explain reform goals, La Solidaridad and publication,
Lopez Jaena/del Pilar/Rizal contributions, La Liga Filipina, an interactive reform
field guide, and a connected report. Five topics have ten medium main variants
and ten separate easy/hard practice questions. The ordinary quiz remains five
questions, passing at three correct, with 50 XP and a mission artifact once.
Normal access requires the student's own earlier mission completions. Local
exploration preview remains enabled for testing, with its existing unsaved behavior.

The lesson defines reform, representation, editor, association and advocacy in
context. It distinguishes a newspaper from an association, a demand from an
achieved result, and reform proposals within Spanish rule from immediate separate
independence. It does not assign identical opinions to all advocates or claim that
one article caused every later event. The Liga is not equated with the Katipunan.

The fifth-scene activity matches publication, organized cooperation and an
outcome-evidence check. Clues unlock matching, which unlocks explanation. Revision
and refresh recovery reuse the existing Firestore-driven exhibit behavior. Six
local SVGs are labelled modern teaching diagrams rather than original documents.
The report frame is reflective guidance; the app does not claim to grade an
independently written historical report.

## Inspected sources

- [NCCA CulturEd: La Solidaridad](https://philippineculturaleducation.com.ph/la-solidaridad/): publication setting, reform demands and contributors. The lesson does not adopt a claim that revolution was the only possible path as an established universal conclusion.
- [NHCP: Graciano Lopez Jaena marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/graciano-lopez-jaena/): first editorship and first-issue date.
- [Provincial Government of Bulacan: Marcelo H. del Pilar](https://bulacan.gov.ph/general-info/marcelo-h-del-pilar/): later editorship. No exact disputed changeover day is assessed.
- [NHCP: La Liga Filipina marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/la-liga-filipina/): 3 July 1892 foundation, aims and weakening after Rizal's arrest/deportation.
- [Rizal: Filipinas dentro de cien anos, Spanish text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/14839/pg14839-images.html): arguments about free press and Philippine representatives. The lesson paraphrases ideas rather than claiming a quotation, a government decree or universal public opinion.

This source-informed draft still needs independent historical, teacher and Grade 5
comprehension review. Editorial mapping locates relevant teaching and assessment;
it does not certify historical completeness or learning effectiveness.
