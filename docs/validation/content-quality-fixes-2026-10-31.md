## Safety and validation

The publisher first compares the full guarded baseline. It saves one complete before-write backup, then applies batches of at most 400 writes using field masks and update-time preconditions. Each batch is atomic; the whole update is not one atomic transaction. An error stops the run without an automatic retry, and a resumed comparison recognizes already-applied records. Only full readback produces a successful publication proof.

Only question wording/options, explanatory fields and scene body text are edited. No learner records, XP, progression, assessment keys, topic IDs, difficulty, passing rules or adaptive evidence policies are changed. Existing content-version safeguards may restart incompatible unfinished drafts. Completed results/rewards are not reset or regraded; past evidence from older questions is not retroactively revalidated as proof of the new tasks.

Five focused tests cover before/after audit findings, source-case coverage, answer/key preservation, supported feedback, exact reading preservation, real challenge availability, scope boundaries, drift rejection and idempotence. The actual quiz renderer checks all 450 revised prompts and four choices; the actual story renderer checks corrected endings and representative reflowed passages alongside existing lesson cases.
