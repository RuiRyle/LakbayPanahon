# Priority 1 - Learning-objective wording review

> Publication update, 7 November 2026: the corrected objective-only migration has now been published and verified against live Firestore. See [publication results](priority-1-publication-summary-2026-11-07.md). Unpublished-status notes below describe the earlier review stage.

Reviewed 7 November 2026. Scope: 13 previously identified missions, containing 65 objective sentences (five per mission). Only learningObjectives authoring was changed.

## What changed and why

The original builders prefixed topic headings with 'Explain' and lowercased them, producing fragments, questions written as objectives, missing possessives, and lowercase proper names. The revised lists use observable student actions, natural Grade 5 wording, and the same five topic outcomes in the same order. They retain each lesson's limits on what evidence can establish. No new event, date, quotation, source, activity, or assessment claim was introduced.

## Preservation and alignment

Each row below is paired with its existing lesson scene and assessment topic. Review included the scene explanation, activity's supported answer, and existing feedback. The objectives describe those existing tasks; they do not create new tasks. Lessons, mission introductions, singular learningObjective summaries, questions/options, scoring, sources, rewards and learner records remain unchanged.

The 13 mission builders now read explicit reviewed objectives from data/mission-objective-wording.json instead of manufacturing them from headings. Original migration files and hash-verified publication history were not rewritten. data/mission-objective-wording-migration.json is a separate, forward-only candidate touching learningObjectives in 13 mission documents, with the expected original lists recorded to detect conflicting edits.

**Publication status:** local authoring and migration preparation only. The live Firestore catalog has not been changed; the live website will continue showing the previous lists until this objective-only migration is reviewed and published. No work on Priorities 2-5 was started.
