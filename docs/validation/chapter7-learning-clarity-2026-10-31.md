## What changed for students

Concrete knowledge remains visible: the Spanish Crown, governor-general/captain-general, issue/delivery/implementation, the Real Audiencia's judicial and other roles, provincial titles, the town-level gobernadorcillo, pueblo, principalia, cabeza de barangay, encomienda, required tribute, and reduccion. The corrupted spelling in the old leadership reading is replaced with **principalia**. Broad terms stay attached to historical settings rather than presented as one unchanged administrative chart for the whole colonial period.

A short source-based example introduces the 1597 Ilocos investigation involving Francisco Salgado. It concerns recorded complaints about collection and pressure, not a universal tax rate or proof that every household experienced the same thing. Separate fictional household models remain labelled. Collection rights and land claims are distinguished without claiming dispossession never occurred.

The variants rotate shared pools of four misconceptions per topic; they are authored teaching items, not independently calibrated measurements of difficulty or learning.
