# Priority 3 — 30-student cost feasibility

7 November 2026. **Read-only review; production remains NO-GO.** Public official documentation and existing local configuration were reviewed. No authenticated production request, billable test, resource/configuration/IAM change, deployment or Priority 4 work occurred. Only this report and its arithmetic/integrity companion JSON were added. Prices are USD before tax/currency conversion; availability of shared allowances is unverified.

## Recommendation

The lowest-risk practical route is the accepted Stage 0 candidate on **Blaze, using its free allowances and zero minimum instances**. Budget **$2–7/month for incremental pilot assessment infrastructure**, not for the whole application. This is a planning estimate with a small contingency, not a guaranteed charge or cap. There is no fixed Blaze subscription fee, but deployment/build/storage and excess usage can be charged. The current four secured Functions cannot be deployed on Spark. Using gcloud instead of Firebase CLI does not remove the billing requirement. [Firebase plans](https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans), [Functions deployment requirement](https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/get-started).

A zero-dollar bill can occur under suitable usage/storage/allowances; **a guaranteed free, no-billing production deployment of this accepted architecture is not available**. Do not use public answer keys, browser grading or permissive XP rules as a cost workaround.

## Explicit 30-student estimate

Retain the earlier educational workload: 20 school days; per student 20 new mission quizzes plus five retries, four chapter challenges, ten daily challenges and 20 restores. Budget 13 callable requests per mission attempt (including follow-ups), three per chapter/daily activity and 10% transport headroom: **12,780 requests/month**. At assumed 0.5–2 active seconds/request, before unmeasured startup/retry overhead, this is 6,390–25,560 vCPU-seconds and 1,598–6,390 GiB-seconds with the accepted 1 CPU/256 MiB configuration. Concurrent requests share instances; this is a conservative accounting proxy, not measured hosted billed time.

Continue using **1.08 million reads and 33,000 writes/month** from the earlier conservative model; it is not a metered post-Option-A invoice. Even distribution gives 54,000 reads/1,650 writes per school day. With otherwise unused daily allowances, 80,000 reads are paid over 20 school days; weekend spare quota does not offset them. The carried-forward Singapore planning read rate $0.0369/100,000 yields about **$0.03**; a $0.03–$0.06/100,000 sensitivity gives $0.024–$0.048. Regional selector/SKU rates must be checked at authorization; the public page's default region is not a verified Singapore quote. Background traffic and storage/index-entry billing can change this.

|---|---:|

| Firestore document reads/writes | About $0.03 with unused daily allowances and the stated read rate; writes fit daily quota |

**Minimum distinction:** Blaze's fixed monthly plan fee is $0. A smaller workload with every allowance available and tiny storage could yield a $0 usage invoice. Under this particular 54K-reads/day and 2-GiB-images model, the conditional core floor is closer to **$0.18/month**, before source storage, backup/recovery and other usage. Expected planning envelope remains **$2–7/month incremental assessment infrastructure**. First-month deployments, acceptance tests and recovery rehearsal can add to it. Do not call either figure a whole-project minimum or promise.

**Important exclusions:** existing/nonpilot users and teachers; Bayani inference/backend/TTS; media volume; all database/index storage beyond free capacity; managed export/restore/PITR and recovery destination; unexpected egress, image scanning, custom metrics, abuse, taxes and currency conversion. Backup scope/data size and current Hosting/media/provider usage are not known, so a responsible all-features whole-project total cannot be fixed from this review. The companion JSON preserves arithmetic, assumptions and source links; no free-trial or education credits are assumed.

App Check attestations are not one per answer submission. Tokens are reused; the SDK refreshes at about half their TTL. Keep the reviewed 3600-second token TTL and score policy; the 1,200–2,400 estimate allows 2–4 attestations per student/school day for sessions/reloads. Extra tabs, devices and other sites can raise it. The assessment attempt's 7200-second expiry is a different setting. [Provider behavior](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-check/web/recaptcha-enterprise-provider).

## Alternatives

| Option | Free feasibility | Security / operational tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted Stage 0 on Blaze | Free-tier-heavy, potentially near-zero usage; billing and overage exposure remain | Smallest change; preserves tested transactions, ADC account separation, callable/Auth/App Check, existing records and protected rollback. Recommended. |
| Spark + static Hosting only | Free within limits | Cannot run these trusted reward Functions. Lessons/review/local demonstrations are possible, but do not deliver the full secured pilot. Leaving the old reward mechanism active is not an acceptable substitute. |
| Spark Firestore/Auth/App Check + free external Node host | Technically plausible under all provider quotas; not demonstrated free end-to-end | Could preserve existing Firestore records and server authority in principle. Requires a separately reviewed deployment/authentication identity design, secret/federation handling, callable transport/CORS/CSP, App Check verification, monitoring and complete regression/hosted acceptance. Render's free service sleeps after 15 idle minutes and may take about a minute to wake; this conflicts with classroom latency/deadlines. The modeled 54K Firestore reads/day also exceeds Spark's basic daily free limit. |
| Cloudflare Workers Free | Published 100K requests/day, 10 ms CPU/request; no demonstrated compatibility for this service | Needs runtime/SDK, identity and transaction compatibility work; cannot assume this Node/Admin/gRPC package is a drop-in. Keep Firestore atomic reward writes; moving balances to another database introduces a major migration/consistency design. |
| School/self-hosted backend | May avoid a new host invoice, not total cost | Electricity, TLS, uptime, patching, credential protection and operator availability remain. Not the accepted workflow; exposing emulators is not a secure production alternative. |
| Google Cloud Run directly / temporary trial credits | Still billing-enabled usage or temporary subsidy | Does not provide a permanent no-billing route; switching service packaging adds work without solving shared database/media costs. |

External hosting is not inherently incapable of secure grading. Firebase supports verifying App Check on custom backends. However, none of these alternatives has demonstrated the required complete compatibility here, and hosting quotas do not eliminate Firestore, attestation, backup or AI costs. No backend switch or record migration is recommended for this pilot. ## Safe savings and bill risks

Keep the accepted min 0/max 2/concurrency 8, two-hour attempts and 3/minute/60/day new-start limits. Preserve same-ID recovery; avoid unnecessary new attempts and unbounded retries. Continue Option A's reduced transaction read scope. Do not remove prerequisite/digest/profile/ownership reads, skip attestation, change rewards, suppress follow-ups or introduce a client fallback.

Before any approved activation, propose alerts and daily first-week review of total project charges, reads, invocation/error/retry counts, image storage, media bytes and provider quota. A $20 alert budget remains a reasonable warning threshold, **not a spending cap**. Under separate approval, consider image/source-artifact retention that preserves reviewed rollback materials; use existing media caches and avoid regenerating unchanged audio. Any new caching/retention/configuration change needs its own review; no learner evidence is deleted. Do not extend App Check TTL for savings or move regions/databases without a separate design.

Unexpected charges can arise from repeated catalog/history queries, transaction retries, refresh/listener loops, valid-user abuse, denied callable requests consuming compute, many image versions/builds, retained attempts/indexes, TTL deletion, frequent full exports, media downloads, paid AI/TTS, SMS, longer-running requests, minimum instances, VPC/NAT or paid observability/scanning. **All-endpoint UID throttling is still absent.** Max instances bounds scale imperfectly; it does not cap cumulative work or the other services' bills. App Check is not a financial cap.

Current official docs also describe **Preview service spend caps**, including underlying Functions services. This is distinct from alerts-only budgets. They can pause a service after reporting delays, incur overages, do not cover every product, and can interrupt classroom saves; applicability to the chosen gcloud-managed resources needs verification. Treat as an optional separately approved investigation, not an already enabled or guaranteed $0 cap. Never automate billing unlinking or delete student data as cost control. [Spend-cap scope and limitations](https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/spend-caps).
