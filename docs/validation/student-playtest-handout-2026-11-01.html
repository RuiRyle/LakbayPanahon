Your LakbayPanahon adventure

Help us see whether the website explains things clearly. You are not being tested. You can ask for help, take a break or stop.

Use the normal website with your supervising adult. Follow the missions available to your account. There are no answers on this sheet.

Try these tasks

  1. Find your first available lesson. Tell your adult what you think you will learn.
  2. Read or listen to the story and try its activities. Explain what one clue helps you understand.
  3. Try the quiz. Then explain what your result means and where you would go next.
  4. If you need to review something, try finding the lesson or practice that can help. It is fine to ask for help.
  5. Find My Journey and something you earned. Explain what it tells you about your journey.

Tell us what you think

  • What was easy to understand?
  • What was confusing?
  • What is one history idea you can explain now?
  • If you used the voice, was it clear? Could you stop it?

You do not need to share passwords, personal information, photos or recordings.

Adult facilitator reminder

This handout contains tasks only. Keep the teacher packet and quiz answer keys away from the learner session.

  • Arrange the required supervision/permission and account process first.
  • Observe the learner's normal route; do not unlock content by editing progress.
  • Do not tell the next click or quiz answer. If help is needed, give it and mark the task assisted.
  • Do not deliberately cause failure. Recovery may be marked not observed.
  • Record anonymous observations only. No names, emails, UIDs, passwords, faces, voices or identifiable screenshots in the project.
  • Stop for distress, misleading content or repeated saving failures.
  • Successful navigation and historical understanding are separate outcomes.

Use the observation template and current preview/local-package instructions in docs/student-review-kit.md. No participant outcomes have been recorded.
