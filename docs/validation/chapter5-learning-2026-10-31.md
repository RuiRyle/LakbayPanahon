The updated lessons retain specific historical knowledge and connect it to reasoning:

- Motives: spices, costs and profit, Spanish sponsorship, Portuguese origin, route sequence, local perspectives and aim versus result.
- Early encounters: Homonhon landfall on 17 March 1521, local help and exchange, Limasawa's recorded service on 31 March, Enrique's interpretation, Cebu arrival on 7 April, Humabon and the recorded baptisms on 14 April. Learners separate communication, religious events and political agreement.
- Mactan: differing choices by Lapulapu and Zula, demands and coercion, Buaya, shoreline conditions, the defenders' victory on 27 April 1521, continued voyages and limits of artistic retellings. A missing identification in an account does not establish either a personal duel or definite absence.
- Later voyages: Victoria and Elcano in 1522; selected departure order Loaisa 1525, Saavedra 1527, Villalobos 1542 with arrival in 1543; Prince Felipe and the naming connection; practical return-route needs; Legazpi's 1565 settlement and the difference between arrival, naming and continuing rule.

## Sources and curriculum limits

For early encounters, the NHCP [Homonhon marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/homonhon-ruta-ng-ekspedisyon-magallanes-elcano-sa-pilipinas/), [Limasawa marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/limasawa-mazaua-ruta-ng-ekspedisyon-magallanes-elcano-sa-pilipinas/) and [Cebu marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/cebu-ruta-ng-ekspedisyon-magallanes-elcano-sa-pilipinas/) support the named events and participants. The Cebu endpoint timed out, but its official indexed marker text was available. The [Mactan marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/mactan-ruta-ng-ekspedisyon-magallanes-elcano-sa-pilipinas/) and NQC's [Battle of Mactan Beyond Textbooks](https://www.nqc.gov.ph/en/resources/battle-of-mactan-beyond-textbooks/) support the dispute, shoreline example and evidence limits; NQC's directly opened page returned 403, with relevant official indexed sections available.

Later voyage checks used the Spanish Army Museum's [Victoria/Elcano return account](https://ejercito.defensa.gob.es/museo/HECHOS_HISTORICOS/HECHOS_HISTORICOS/09.06_sept_SE_COMPLETO_LA_PRIMERA_VUELTA_AL_MUNDO_POR_ELCANO.html), the Spanish Navy's [Loaisa overview](https://armada.defensa.gob.es/ArmadaPortal/page/Portal/ArmadaEspannola/cienciaorgano/prefLang-es/02cienciaihcn--10navegaciones--07expgarciajofredeloaisa) and [Villalobos overview](https://armada.defensa.gob.es/ArmadaPortal/page/Portal/ArmadaEspannola/cienciaorgano/prefLang-en/02cienciaihcn--10navegaciones--20expvillalobos), its [naval history journal](https://publicaciones.defensa.gob.es/media/downloadable/files/links/r/h/rhn_045.pdf) for Saavedra, and the NCCA [Legazpi/return-route account](https://ncca.gov.ph/about-culture-and-arts/in-focus/philippines-gateway-to-the-orient-from-legazpi-to-malaspina/). Some directly opened sources were unavailable; official indexed passages were used and original lesson claims retained rather than expanding uncertain detail. The preserved naming connection still requires independent teacher/source review; no new claim of unanimous local adoption is made.

The naming connection is also supported by the Spanish Navy's [2022 historical monograph, volume 86](https://armada.defensa.gob.es/archivo/mardigitalrevistas/cuadernosihcn/86cuaderno/Cuaderno86Completo.pdf), whose official indexed passage identifies the name honoring the emperor's heir. This supports retaining the modest naming connection without adopting the inaccurate title “reigning king” found in some summaries of the 1540s.

Grade 5 MATATAG mapping remains provisional. The 13 existing partial-depth findings are still open; editorial revisions do not constitute curriculum certification or a learner study.
