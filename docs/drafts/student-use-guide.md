## For students

### Explore and learn

Read the story scenes and open the learning clues. Some scenes ask you to make a choice or explain an idea. Take your time and look for evidence in the lesson.

Read the quiz instructions before you begin. Your result shows your next step. If a topic needs more work, revisit its explanation and try the recommended practice. Return to the main quiz when you are ready.

Practice helps you build confidence. The practice goal is a sign of progress; keep using what you learned in later lessons.

### Keep your discoveries

My Journey shows saved progress and learning suggestions. Save opened clues to your Evidence Journal when you want to find them again. Removing a bookmark does not remove your lesson progress.

Completed missions can award rewards once. Reviewing a completed lesson helps you remember it. Read the saved/saving messages before leaving an activity; an internet problem can stop your latest place from saving.

### Learn with your class

Use a class code from your teacher to join the intended class. Check the teacher's assignment instructions and due date. If you leave a class, your saved learning progress stays in your account.

### Look after your account

Keep your password private. Ask a trusted adult when you need account help. Log out when you finish on a shared computer. Your sound and reading choices may remain in that browser.

### Use AI carefully

**Operator review: learner-facing AI use is not approved by this draft. Publish this section only after the provider and privacy arrangements are resolved.**

Bayani AI is an AI assistant. It can help explain a history idea, but it can make mistakes. Check explanations against your lesson and ask a trusted adult if something seems wrong or upsetting.

Try asking for a hint or an example. Give your own answers during a quiz. Keep your address, phone number, passwords and other private details out of chat. New Chat clears the conversation on the page; an outside AI service may handle its own records separately.
