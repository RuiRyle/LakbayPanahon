## Learning change

Chapter 15 Mission 4 still has six scenes and five quiz topics. Scene 1 now defines
exemption and grievance and introduces the reported cause. Scene 3 compares two
named accounts using a second interactive matching board, followed by explanation.
Scene 6 revisits causes, extent, sequence and later remembrance. Two main variants
and two easy/hard practice questions now assess this comparison under the existing
accusation-and-evidence topic ID. The original fifth-scene remembrance board stays.
Two new labelled SVG teaching diagrams support the updated content.

The comparison keeps these distinctions:

- Plauchut's later account links the uprising to lost tax/required-labor exemptions,
  describes privileges connected with retirement, and also reports expected support
  that did not arrive. It is not reduced to a claim that no planning existed.
- Izquierdo's contemporary report presents suppression as stopping plans prepared
  in advance against Spanish rule. An official source remains an account to examine.
- Explaining a grievance or a wider plan does not itself establish who organized
  the event. Neither author is treated as the voice of an entire nationality.

Students match each source with its emphasis and the limit on the comparison,
then explain it. Clues, correction feedback, revision and draft recovery use the
existing Firestore-driven activity behavior. No violence or execution reenactment
is introduced. An invented classroom payment example explains a term without
inventing historical wages, tax amounts or quotations.

## Sources actually inspected

- [Rafael de Izquierdo, report dated 31 January 1872, printed in Gaceta de Madrid, 24 March 1872](https://www.boe.es/gazeta/dias/1872/03/24/pdfs/GMD-1872-84.pdf): report on PDF pages 2-4 (one-based), including prepared-plan language and the Fort San Felipe setting. Text and a page screenshot were requested; no complete trial file is claimed.
- [Edmond Plauchut, L'Archipel des Philippines, 1877, third installment, section IV](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/L%E2%80%99Archipel_des_Philippines/03): French primary text, especially paragraphs on lost exemptions/retirement privileges and hoped-for support. Browser lesson summaries are our English paraphrases, not quotations or a published translation. No eyewitness status is claimed.

NHCP's Two Faces article and the full Schumacher PDF could not be retrieved;
this enrichment does not pretend to have read them. The comparison uses the
accessible primary texts instead. Graphic passages and period racial language
are not reproduced for children. Direct source URLs remain metadata for review.
