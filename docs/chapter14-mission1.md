Six scenes distinguish contributions from outcomes, examine community organization in the Dagohoy movement, identify Gabriela Silang's leadership continuation, separate Basi remembrance from immediate outcomes, and build a supported historical lesson. This opening synthesis revisits named cases through a new question rather than claiming every early revolt already shared a national program. Later missions examine regions, participation, unity and changing identity.

Each scene has three clues, a decision and authored Explain help. Five labelled modern diagrams are not presented as original historical images. No battle reenactments, graphic imagery or rewards for harm are added.

Sources and boundaries:

- [CulturEd: Francisco Dagohoy](https://philippineculturaleducation.com.ph/dagohoy-francisco/): communities, local organization and continuation beyond the founder. Movement dates are not a lifespan, and long duration is not proof that every aim was achieved.
- [NHCP registry: Gabriela Silang marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/maria-josefa-gabriela-silang-1723-1763/): the specific continuation claim and later marker date. The lesson does not use the disputed birth year or the marker's sweeping first-woman claim. A marker is not a complete participant list or transcript; roles are not assigned from gender or ancestry.

Recognition does not establish immediate victory. No historical paintings or graphic scenes are reproduced.
