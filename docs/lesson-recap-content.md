# Mission recap content

Prepared from existing mission introductions, objectives and assessment explanations. Independent curriculum review remains pending.

## Life in the Barangay

Barangay life involved local leadership, different social roles, customs and cooperation. Communities could organize themselves in different ways.

- Describe a barangay as an organized community.
- Explain how leadership, customs and cooperation could support community life.
- Recognize that social roles and organization varied among communities.

## Trade and Livelihood

Farming, fishing and craft skills helped communities use local resources for daily needs. Trade connected communities with different products and resources.

- Connect farming and fishing to local environments and food needs.
- Explain how craft skills produced useful objects.
- Describe how exchanging products could meet different community needs.

## Roles and Responsibilities in Early Filipino Communities

Community members had different roles and responsibilities. Leadership, family connections, customs and cooperation could support daily life, while social positions could affect rights and duties.

- Identify leadership and community responsibilities.
- Explain how relatives, customs and cooperation supported community life.
- Recognize that rights, duties and freedom could differ by social position.

## Beliefs and Cultural Traditions

Beliefs and traditions differed among early Philippine communities. Ritual specialists, ceremonies and oral traditions could support spiritual and cultural life. Artifacts offer clues that require careful interpretation.

- Describe examples of spiritual roles, beliefs and ceremonies.
- Explain how oral traditions passed knowledge between generations.
- Recognize cultural diversity and interpret artifacts with historical context.

## Islam and Cultural Exchange

Trade, travel and teaching connected communities and helped Islam spread gradually to some parts of the Philippines. Families, teachers and leaders contributed to Muslim community life, including in Sulu and parts of Mindanao.

- Explain how contact and teaching contributed to the spread of Islam.
- Identify kindness, charity and care for others among the teachings discussed.
- Describe community development and leadership while recognizing diverse Philippine traditions.
