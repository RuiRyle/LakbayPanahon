## The Philippines as an Archipelago: Location and Geography

Mission: era1-prologue / prologue-mission1 ? Related topics: Q1.5

**Big question:** How can a map help explain connections between island communities?

**Why it matters:** Geography helps us ask how people travelled and met their needs. A map gives clues, not a complete account of their lives.

**Apply it:** A map shows an inland river meeting the coast. Which explanation uses that clue?

- The river could connect inland and coastal places.
- The river identifies every traveller.
- Water means people could never meet.

<details>
<summary>Reviewer: answer and teaching feedback</summary>

- Choice 1 (supported answer): The river offers a possible connection. We need further evidence to explain particular journeys.
- Choice 2: A route on a map does not identify who used it.
- Choice 3: Water can separate places and provide a route between them.

</details>
