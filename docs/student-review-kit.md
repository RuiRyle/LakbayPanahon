## Adult content reviewer: first 15–20 minutes

Read the current first mission and its feedback in the website before inviting learners. Check that the objective is clear, each question is taught in the lesson, images are labelled accurately, fictional activities are distinguishable from historical records, and wrong answers explain the relevant idea without shaming the learner.

Then start Q1.1 in the [current 13-target packet](learning-review-packet.md). It contains current lesson passages and assessment samples, plus the earlier editorial concern. The original 28 October labels are not a new judgment of the revised content. Use [the first clarity proposal](history-sources-clarity-proposal.md) as a small concrete teaching revision to evaluate. Do not mark all 13 targets suitable from one reviewed example.

Record for each target:

| Reference / exact scene or question | Claim or wording checked | Source checked | Specific correction / rationale | Outcome | Reviewer / date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | Needs correction / suitable for supervised playtest / specialist input needed | |

Keep assessment keys in the adult packet. The short source-analysis method reference is the [National Archives document-analysis guidance](https://www.archives.gov/education/teaching-with-documents); it is not a Philippine curriculum endorsement. The curriculum mapping remains the project's provisional August 2023 Grade 5 MATATAG comparison, not DepEd certification.

## Facilitator preparation

- Use the [printable student PDF](validation/student-playtest-handout-2026-11-01.pdf) or [HTML handout](validation/student-playtest-handout-2026-11-01.html), which have no answer keys. Arrange required supervision/permission and the actual account-handling process before a learner session.
- Explain: “We are checking whether the website explains itself clearly. This is not a test of you. You can pause or stop.” Do not promise that participating proves learning gains.
- The learner uses their agreed, normal account route. Student learning does not require a teacher class. Do not create or edit learner progress to unlock later content.
- Do not record names, emails, account UIDs, passwords, faces, voice, chat transcripts or identifiable screenshots in this repository. Use `Learner 01` only; keep any necessary participant/consent administration outside the repository under the responsible operator's process.
- Do not show quiz answers, coach the next click, or deliberately make a learner fail. If help is needed, give it kindly and mark that task assisted. Stop for distress, repeated saving failure or misleading/sensitive content.

## Learner session: approximately 20 minutes

1. Start at the normal landing/sign-in route. Ask the learner to find the first available lesson. Note whether they locate the next action without coaching.
2. Let them read/listen and use the lesson activities. Ask once: “What does this clue help you understand?” Record a brief anonymous paraphrase and the relevant concept, not a transcript.
3. Let them complete the quiz normally. Ask what they think the result and next action mean. Verify a saved result/acknowledgement rather than assuming a click saved.
4. If the learner naturally needs review, observe whether they can find the mapped lesson/practice/retry. Otherwise mark recovery not observed and demonstrate it separately with an adult-owned account; do not manufacture a child's failure.
5. Ask them to find My Journey and a saved reward/collection. Ask what progress/reward means. If narration was used, ask whether it was understandable and whether they knew how to stop it.

Later-chapter tasks are optional and require legitimately eligible accounts. Use the adult content packet when a chapter is locked; leave learner comprehension of it unobserved. Do not seed a learner's records, share someone else's credentials or restore exploration mode.

## Anonymous observation sheet

Use [the blank CSV](validation/student-playtest-observations-template.csv). No rows have been filled with results.

| Task | Outcome | What to observe |
|---|---|---|
| Find first mission | Unaided / assisted / not completed / not observed | Label the learner chose and any confusion about the next step. |
| Explain a clue | Accurate / partial / incorrect / not observed | Does the explanation use the taught idea/evidence, rather than repeat a label? |
| Complete lesson activity | Unaided / assisted / not completed / not observed | Clear instructions, text load, image readability and navigation. |
| Understand quiz result | Accurate / partial / incorrect / not observed | Pass/review meaning, saving acknowledgement and next action. |
| Recover after a mistake | Unaided / assisted / not completed / not observed | Only if it happens naturally; mark unobserved otherwise. |
| Explain progress/reward | Accurate / partial / incorrect / not observed | Difference between completing work, XP/rewards and learning the idea. |
| Use narration | Clear / unclear / not used | Human listening and usable start/stop; no recording needed. |

Keep usability and understanding separate. “Clicked successfully” is not evidence of historical understanding. An unobserved task is not a failed or passed task. A few participants do not establish long-term effectiveness, national suitability or complete curriculum coverage.

## Turn observations into the next fix

For each problem, record exact page/mission/control, what was observed, expected behavior, severity and proposed small change. Prioritize access/save failures and misleading historical teaching, then repeated navigation/comprehension problems, then visual polish. Do not store private learner answers as debugging material. Retest the changed task and affected regression checks; avoid re-running unrelated suites after everything passes.
