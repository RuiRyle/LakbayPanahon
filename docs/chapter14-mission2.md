# Chapter 14 Mission 2: Participation of Different Regions and Communities

> Publication update - 28 October 2026: This release is now published to Firestore, backed up and field-verified. See [Chapters 7-14 publication](chapters7-14-publication.md). Older status sections below record the original authoring stage.

Locally authored and checked on 2026-10-28. Not published to Firestore or Hosting.

Six scenes distinguish location from participation, compare separate Bohol movements, examine the reported reach of the Sumuroy uprising, identify a named Ilocos contribution and build a regional evidence portfolio. Students organize case, place, period, recorded action and limits rather than merge all residents into one participant group. Later missions examine women and Indigenous peoples, unity and changing identity more closely.

Each scene has three clues, a decision and authored Explain help. Five modern labelled diagrams are teaching cards, not participant registers or reconstructions of exact historical routes. Fictional household and action cards explain evidence categories without inventing historical residents or speeches. No identity disclosure, compulsory religious practice or harm-scoring task is used.

Sources and boundaries:

- [CulturEd: Tamblot](https://philippineculturaleducation.com.ph/tamblot/): Bohol, 1621 and the account's religious-practice concern. Limited biographical information is not filled with invented dialogue. Graphic passages and uncertain participant totals are not reproduced.
- [CulturEd: Francisco Dagohoy](https://philippineculturaleducation.com.ph/dagohoy-francisco/): the separate Bohol movement dated 1744-1829. A common island does not establish identical participants or coordination across centuries. Movement dates are not a lifespan.
- [NHCP registry: Agustin Sumuroy](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/agustin-sumuroy/): Palapag, 1 June 1649 and the marker's reported spread into areas of the Visayas, Mindanao and Luzon. This is attributed to a later marker, not treated as a participant census, evidence that every community joined, or an exact route reconstruction. The marker's sweeping first-revolt claim and graphic ending are excluded.
- [NHCP registry: Gabriela Silang](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/maria-josefa-gabriela-silang-1723-1763/): the specific continuation claim in Ilocos. Disputed birth-year and first-woman claims are not used. A leader biography is not a complete regional account.
