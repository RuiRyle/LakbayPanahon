Every main topic has an explanation in the candidate lessons. This does not prove that all historical dates/interpretations are accurate, that retry items have equal difficulty, or that children understand the explanations.

There are many abstract distinctions and source-limit statements; learner observation should check comprehension rather than adding further caveats automatically. The non-graphic treatment of conflict and distinction between fictional prompts and original speeches should be retained.

## Concrete stale-guidance issue

The assembled chapter still contains two early-development endings: Mission 1 says Chapter 5 is “still being built”; Mission 2 says it “remains unfinished.” Later plans add the remaining missions and chapter challenge, so this wording can mislead a learner about available content.

`data/chapter5-continuation-wording-migration.json` replaces those endings with next-lesson guidance and explains that a chapter badge requires completing the missions and passing the chapter challenge. It does not assert that the current student has completed anything.

Only two scene bodies change. Historical explanations, correct answers, interactions, source metadata, images and reward rules are unchanged. Live guards require matching original bodies and active later missions/challenge; a mismatch must stop publication for review.
