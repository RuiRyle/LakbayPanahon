# Chapter 13 Mission 4: Local Grievances and Growing Political Awareness

> Publication update - 28 October 2026: This release is now published to Firestore, backed up and field-verified. See [Chapters 7-14 publication](chapters7-14-publication.md). Older status sections below record the original authoring stage.

Locally authored and checked on 2026-10-28. Not published to Firestore or Hosting.

Six scenes connect specific local concerns with questions about authority and participation. Students compare the earlier Bacolor petition, production complaints under the tobacco monopoly and the Basi uprising, then build a report with one supported connection and one limit. The synthesis deliberately revisits known cases with a new question: what do recorded actions show about political awareness, and what broader claims still need evidence?

Each scene includes three clues, a decision and authored Explain help. Five labelled modern teaching diagrams support the activities. The classroom reading-club example is explicitly fictional; no invented historical speech, household experience or participant list is presented as evidence.

Sources and boundaries:

- [UP Diliman research summary: They Are Not Foreigners](https://history.upd.edu.ph/publications/they-are-not-foreigners/): the 1698 Bacolor request and land-policy pressure. This is clearly an earlier comparison, not a response to later rights documents. The lesson does not infer independence aims, a successful request or shared motives from ancestry. The department abstract, not the complete archival dossier, was consulted.
- [National Tobacco Administration historical account](https://www.nta.da.gov.ph/tobacco/): compulsory production, quotas and complaints in affected areas. Income and household wellbeing are separate questions. Promotional language, stereotypes and graphic punishment are excluded; tobacco use or production is not a learning task.
- [CCP Encyclopedia: Basi Revolt](https://epa.culturalcenter.gov.ph/3/82/2142/): the policy connection in the 1807 Ilocos case. The lesson paraphrases the limited economic-policy connection and excludes graphic imagery, reenactments and contested detailed event chronology. Alcohol use is not encouraged.
- Political awareness is a teaching definition concerning rules, authority and participation. Claims of growth require comparison across dated records. Local complaints are not treated as proof of universal national identity, a coordinated national program or inevitable later events. Claims of international influence still require evidence of receipt and response from Mission 3.

The main quiz selects five medium questions across five topics from ten equivalent variants. Ten separate easy/hard practice questions use existing adaptive review policy. Passing requires three correct answers. The finite bank does not promise unlimited unseen retries. The existing one-time reward flow is configured for 50 XP and a Local Concerns Evidence Journal token.
