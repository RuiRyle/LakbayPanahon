ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Making Philippine History More Engaging

LakbayPanahon is an AI powered gamified Philippine History learning system designed for Grade 5 students. It combines interactive learning content, story based missions, quizzes, gamification, and AI assisted learning support.

Students can learn independently or join a teacher created class for additional classroom support and progress monitoring.
