A CLASSROOM EXPLORATION ROUTINE

Turn a history lesson into a discovery.

  1. Set the scene. Ask students what they already know about the lesson's people, place, or period.
  2. Look for evidence. Have students read the story and explain what a clue suggests.
  3. Talk it through. Invite students to compare ideas and support an explanation with evidence.
  4. Return and reflect. Ask students to revisit their Evidence Journal and explain one thing they learned.
